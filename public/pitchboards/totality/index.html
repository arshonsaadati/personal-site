Totality

Totality uses roguelike mechanics to gamify your to-do list and track progress on long-term goals.

This board goes through the ideas so far: the map, the daily loop, the wager, and the devices around it. It shows the direction for now.

How the map works

An act is two weeks long and is laid out like a star chart. Each day is a star, and the route taken through the days gets drawn in like a constellation. Below is a mockup of what the wall frame would show on day 8.

  • Sun: health

    Each ray is one hit point. Rays go out when tasks are left unfinished. The dashed ring is a shield, which comes from healing past full health.

  • Moon: act clock

    The moon fills in one step per day. An act runs from new moon to full moon.

  • Filled stars: finished days

    A filled star is a day that has been played. The solid line joining them is the route taken so far.

  • Star size: stance

    The size of a star shows the stance picked that day. Lean is small, full is medium, and stretch is large with rays.

  • Dotted lines: this week’s routes

    At the start of each week you pick one of the routes. The dashed line, α, is the one picked here.

  • Planet symbols: day types

    A symbol next to a star shows what that day offers, such as a market or a pact. These are visible ahead of time.

  • Shaded bands: calendar

    Days with a lot of meetings are shaded. Here route α has a bounty day under Thursday’s shading, and route β has a rest day.

  • Eclipse: boss day

    The last day of the act is the boss day, which is a hard challenge. All routes end there.

The core loop

You set a long-term goal, such as getting better at drawing. An LLM breaks it down into daily tasks, and your own tasks and calendar events get added in. From there the game runs on four timescales.

  • Day

    Each day’s tasks are that day’s fight. Every task has HP based on its size. Tasks left unfinished deal damage at the end of the day.

  • Week

    At the start of each week you pick a route through it. Routes differ in what each day offers.

  • Act

    An act is two weeks and ends with a boss day. The moon shows how far into the act you are.

  • Run

    A run starts with a real-money wager. It ends when the boss is beaten or your health runs out.

The wager

Each run starts with a real-money wager. What happens to the money depends on how the run ends.

If you win

The money goes toward a treat

Something like a cool shirt or a dinner out.

If you die

The money goes into your investment account

It is invested and the treat is skipped.

Ascension. Winning a run makes the next one harder. Dying drops you down a rank.

A day

The tasks for a day are treated as one enemy, called a minion. Its health is the combined HP of the tasks that are still unfinished.

  1. The night before

    Tasks for the next day are entered. Each task gets HP based on its size.

  2. Morning

    You pick a stance for the day: lean, full, or stretch.

  3. During the day

    Finishing tasks takes health off the minion. Good habits heal you, like making the bed, skin care, or hitting a step goal.

  4. End of day

    Any HP the minion has left is dealt to you as damage. The day’s star is then filled in on the chart.

  • Lean

    Must-do tasks only. Lower payout.

  • Full

    All of the day’s tasks.

  • Stretch

    Extra work toward the long-term goal. Higher payout.

Day types

Every day has a fight. Some days also come with something extra, shown on the chart with a planet symbol. A week’s routes differ in which of these days they include.

  • MercuryMarket

    A shop where gold can be spent.

  • VenusRest

    A lighter day that heals.

  • MarsBounty

    Tougher rules for a bigger payout.

  • JupiterPerk

    A perk can be earned.

  • SaturnPact

    A pact is offered.

  • CometMystery

    A random event comes up.

  • EclipseBoss

    The hard challenge at the end of the act.

  • Plain starFight

    A normal day. Pays gold only.

What you collect during a run

These build up over the course of a run and reset when it ends.

  • Gold and the vice shop

    Finishing days earns gold. Gold is spent in a vice shop that you stock yourself, with things like nicotine, takeout, or a gaming night.

  • Perks

    Passive bonuses that last for the run and bend a rule. For example, weekends deal half damage, or the first task finished before nine counts double.

  • Consumables

    Single-use items with limited slots, such as a skip token, a deadline extension, or a reroll of the next day’s options.

  • Pacts

    A restriction you agree to in exchange for a payoff. For example, no phone before noon for a week, and each day pays more gold.

Mystery days

Some days on the chart are marked with a comet. Those are mystery days. On a mystery day a random event comes up that changes a rule for the day or offers a trade. Events are drawn from a pool, and the ones below are examples of the kind of thing that could be in it.

Example events

A deal
Trade some health for a consumable, or gold for a perk.
A bet
Finish the day’s tasks by six for double gold. Miss it and take extra damage.
A windfall
A free consumable, or a day of half damage.
A twist
Habits heal double for the day.
A side quest
Something from your own someday list comes up as an optional task.

The ecosystem

The game is meant to run across an app and two e-ink devices. They sync with each other, so the same game state shows up on all three.

  • The app

    Where goals, tasks, the wager, and the weekly route are set up and managed.

  • The wall frame

    An e-ink picture frame that shows the star chart and how the current day is going.

  • The planner

    An e-ink planner that syncs with the app. It shows the day’s tasks and lets you add new ones.
